About Skyview Academy

Education that goes beyond the classroom.

Skyview Academy is an online education and mentorship initiative committed to supporting learners and students throughout their academic journey.

We provide structured academic support, personalised tutoring, mentorship, career guidance and life-skills development.

From Grade 4 through university, we help learners build the knowledge, confidence and skills they need to succeed.
What We Offer

Programmes designed for every stage of the learning journey.

Our programmes combine academic support with mentorship, personal development and career preparation.

01 — SCHOOL

Primary & High School Tutoring

Personalised tutoring and academic support for learners from Grade 4 to Grade 12, helping them strengthen their understanding, improve performance and develop effective study habits.

02 — UNIVERSITY

Tertiary Academic Support

Academic assistance for university and college students, including subject support, assignment guidance, research assistance and academic development.

03 — CAREERS

Career Guidance & Employability

Career guidance, CV development, interview preparation and employability support to help young people make informed career decisions and prepare for the world of work.

04 — MENTORSHIP

Mentorship & Life Skills

Structured mentorship focused on confidence, leadership, goal setting, communication, personal development and responsible decision-making.
