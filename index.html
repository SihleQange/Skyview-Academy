About Skyview Academy

Education support that goes beyond the classroom.

Skyview Academy provides holistic academic and personal development support through a fully online model. The Academy combines personalised tutoring, research support, mentorship, life skills and career guidance to support learners through different stages of education and into the world of work.

Our Vision

To be South Africa’s leading centre for academic excellence and holistic learner development, empowering students from all backgrounds to achieve their highest potential.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, learner-centred academic and personal development support from primary to university level, equipping learners with skills, confidence and guidance to excel academically, transition successfully into higher education and thrive in their chosen careers.

What We Offer

Four areas of support. One holistic approach.

Our programmes are designed around the academic, developmental and career needs of learners and students at different stages of their journey.

01

Primary & High School Tutoring

Goal: Strengthen foundational skills and improve academic outcomes.

  • Grades 4–7: Mathematics, English and Natural Sciences
  • Grades 8–12: Mathematics, Physics, Life Sciences, Economics and English
  • Study skills and exam preparation workshops
  • Reading comprehension and creative writing development
  • Continuous assessment and learner progress tracking
02

Tertiary Academic Support

Goal: Enhance academic success and research capacity at higher education level.

  • Research proposal and dissertation support
  • Academic writing, referencing and presentation workshops
  • Data analysis and interpretation support
  • Time management and productivity coaching
03

Career Guidance & Employability

Goal: Prepare learners for informed academic and career decisions.

  • Career assessments and counselling
  • University application and scholarship guidance
  • CV, cover letter and interview preparation
  • Professional and soft skills workshops
04

Mentorship & Life Skills

Goal: Develop confident, resilient and purpose-driven individuals.

  • Academic and professional mentor pairing
  • Leadership, communication and goal-setting
  • Emotional intelligence and life skills
  • Motivational sessions and youth dialogues

Sustainability & Access

A sustainable model with inclusion at its core.

Skyview Academy combines paid and subsidised services to support long-term sustainability while widening access to quality education.

Revenue Streams

  • Tutoring and mentorship session fees
  • Research support and editing services
  • Paid workshops, bootcamps and study camps
  • Institutional partnerships with schools and universities
  • Corporate sponsorships for subsidised learners

Subsidy Model

A portion of paid programme income will fund scholarships and fee reductions for learners from low-income households, supporting equitable access to quality education.
